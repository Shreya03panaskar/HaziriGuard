How the numbers work (Transparent Math & Safety Formulas) ▾

1. Integer Cross-Multiplication

Computers suffer from floating-point rounding errors (e.g. 0.1 + 0.2 ≠ 0.3). HaziriGuard strictly compares whole integers:

belowLimit = (100 * attended) < (required * total)

2. Safety Buffer & Emergency Reserve

Safe misses represent how many classes you could miss before dropping below your required threshold. The emergency reserve (default 3) is always withheld:

safeMisses  = floor((100*a - r*t) / r)
spareBuffer = max(0, safeMisses - reserve)

3. Recovery Needed

When attendance drops below threshold, this calculates the exact number of consecutive upcoming classes you must attend to restore your requirement:

recoveryNeeded = ceil((r*t - 100*a) / (100 - r))

Worked Example (Judge Verification Test Case):

36 attended out of 40 held at 75% required (Emergency Reserve = 3):

  • Current Percentage: 100 * 36 / 40 = 90.0%
  • Safe Misses: floor((3600 - 3000) / 75) = floor(600 / 75) = 8 classes
  • Safety Buffer (Spare): 8 safe misses - 3 emergency reserve = 5 spare classes
  • Status: Safe (5 classes buffer beyond emergency reserve)
  • Recovery Needed: 0 (not below threshold)

Important: Rules differ by college. Always check your official attendance record.
